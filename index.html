What we do

If you can hook a truck to it, we can move it.

We provide semi tractors and/or trailers to move your equipment in 49 states and all Canadian provinces, plus drivers to drive your equipment. Entertainment logistics is our specialty.

🎸

Tour & Entertainment

Tour and concert trucks, event logistics, staging, lighting and sound, catering and dressing room trailers, trade shows, sporting events, experiential marketing campaigns and mobile stages.

🚛

Power Only

Our drivers and tractors move your trailers: leased or customer-owned, preloaded trailer shipments, generator trailers, ice floor trailers and winter sports equipment.

↔️

Towaway / Driveaway

"Move my trailer" requests, trailer relocation, delivery and one-way equipment moves. Also called drop and hook or drive away.

⛑️

Disaster Relief

Since 1995: 9/11 and Hurricanes Katrina, Sandy, Nate, Harvey, Irma and Sally, plus floods, fires and mudslides. Generators, chillers, mobile medical units, command centers and more.

About

Rodney McDonald, President

Rodney opened his first company at 20 and learned Murphy's Law the hard way: anything that can go wrong, will. What matters is how you handle the problem, and how you plan for the unknown.

For more than 31 years he has handled last-minute, surprise and immediate-need transportation for sports and entertainment. CW Leasing & Transport (est. 1990) and sister company CW Logistics (est. 2001), which adds capacity through partner and dedicated carriers, are family businesses that Rodney and Christi were fortunate to buy and carry forward.
